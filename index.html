01 / Overview

Gravity,
without limits

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is so intense that nothing, not even light, can escape once it crosses the boundary known as the event horizon
Most form when massive stars collapse at the end of their lives

02 / Anatomy

Three layers
of no return

Singularity

The core, where known physics breaks down and density is predicted to become infinite

Event Horizon

The point of no return
Escaping would require travelling faster than light

Accretion Disk

Superheated matter spiralling inward, glowing across the spectrum before it vanishes

04 / Exploration

Seeing the
invisible

We cannot see black holes directly
We observe their effects: orbiting stars, glowing disks, gravitational waves and shadows captured by telescope networks

  • 2015 LIGO detects gravitational waves from merging black holes
  • 2019 Event Horizon Telescope images M87*
  • 2022 Sagittarius A* is imaged at the Milky Way's center
